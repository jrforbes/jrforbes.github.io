It was great to attend IROS 2026 in Pittsburgh last week! :smiley:

[Mitchell Cohen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mitchell-cohen-20b18a156) presented the paper "Observability and Consistency Analysis for Visual-Inertial Navigation with Anchored Feature Parameterizations", co-authored by [Vassili Korotkine](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vkorotkine) and myself. [Photos!](https://lnkd.in/p/gWsk5Qbq) [arXiv!](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19307)

[Zi Cong (Daniel) Guo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/zi-cong-daniel-guo) presented the paper "KILO-EKF: Koopman-Inspired Learned Observations Extended Kalman Filter", co-authored by myself and [Timothy Barfoot](https://www.linkedin.com/in/timothy-barfoot). [Photos!](https://lnkd.in/p/gvyXerJ4) [arXiv!](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12463)

As part of the [Perception and Navigation for Marine Robotics](https://www.marinerobotics.info/) workshop, [Arturo Del Castillo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/arturodcb) presented "Revisiting Forward-Looking Sonar Image Registration", co-authored by [John Jung](https://www.linkedin.com/in/john-jung-b39020115), [Junha Yoo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/junhayoo), and myself. [Photos](https://lnkd.in/p/gWDwVq2y)

It was a pleasure to be a keynote speaker in the [Data in Field Robotics: From State Estimation to Navigation](https://jonasfrey96.github.io/data_in_field_robotics/) workshop at #IROS2026, where I spoke about the DECAR groups on-going subsea navigation and SLAM work.
