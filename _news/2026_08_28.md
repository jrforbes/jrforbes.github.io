This past week DECAR team members [Arturo Del Castillo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/arturodcb/), [Angad Bajwa](https://www.linkedin.com/in/angad-bajwa/), and [Junha Yoo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/junhayoo/) were collecting ROV data on the Welland Canal! Stereo camera, forward looking sonar, DVL, and depth data will be used to further test our latest :ocean: subsea :ocean: SLAM pipeline, [DIVO](https://lnkd.in/gFBXJwPw ). Huge thanks to our partners [Voyis](https://voyis.com/), [OPG](https://www.opg.com/), [NESRC](https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en), and [Mitacs](https://www.mitacs.ca/), for supporting this research! And a special shout out to [Colin Beal](https://www.linkedin.com/in/colin-beal/), ``ROV operator extraordinaire!"
