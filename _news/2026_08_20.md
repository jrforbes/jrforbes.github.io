Today [Caileigh Bates](https://www.linkedin.com/in/caileigh-bates-302152230/) and [Charles Morin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/charles-morin-85408b2b0/) presented their summer research as part of [McGill Faculty of Engineering](https://www.linkedin.com/company/faculty-of-engineering-mcgill)'s Summer Undergraduate Research in Engineering (SURE) program. :smiley:

Caileigh Bates, co-advied by [Joshua Marshall](https://www.linkedin.com/in/botprof/), presented her work entitled "Feasibility of Surface-Based Magnetic Induction Localization in Mines."

Charles Morin presented his work entitled "Robot Navigation using an Iterated Extended Kalman Filter."
